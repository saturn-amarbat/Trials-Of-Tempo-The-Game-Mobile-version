Privacy Policy — Trials of Tempo

Last updated: February 2026

Data Collection

Trials of Tempo does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All game progress (scores, settings, purchases) is stored locally on your device using localStorage and is never sent to us or any third party.

Third-Party Services

This app uses Google AdMob to display optional rewarded advertisements. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data according to their own privacy policy. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue at our GitHub repository.
